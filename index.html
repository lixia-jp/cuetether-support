FOR THE PERSON LEADING THE ROOM

A timing tool for real workshops.

For facilitators, trainers, and teachers who need to decide what happens next—not just watch a countdown.

Protect essential time

Set a minimum for each segment or protect its full duration. Leave room for an exercise, a break, or a closing discussion.

Review before you adjust

See a proposal that shortens future segments in proportion to their available flexibility. Apply it yourself. If the plan cannot fit, the remaining shortfall stays visible.

Keep the next session grounded

Save and duplicate agendas. After a session, review its history and export the timing record as text or CSV.

You lead.
The timer follows.

Move to the next segment when you are ready. Pause when you need to. The original finish target remains visible, so interruptions do not quietly move the goalposts.

CueTether supports your timing decisions. It does not automatically skip segments or guarantee an on-time finish.

Built for your device

  • Agendas and session history are saved locally.
  • A saved session can be recovered after reopening the app.
  • Optional local reminders depend on your notification and Focus settings.
  • No account, advertising, subscription, or in-app purchases.
