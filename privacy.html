Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026 · Applies to CueTether 1.0.0

CueTether keeps your agendas and session history on your device. The app does not send this content to us and has no advertising, analytics SDKs, account system, or remote server.

What stays on your device

Agenda names, segment names, planned and minimum durations, protection settings, active-session state, and timing history are saved locally so you can reopen and reuse them. CueTether does not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, recordings, or usage analytics.

Exports and backups

When you choose to export a timing record, you decide where to save or share the text or CSV export through the system sharing interface. That destination may have its own privacy policy. Exported copies remain at the destination until you delete them there.

App data may be included in device backups according to your Apple device and backup settings. CueTether does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service. Reinstalling the app alone does not restore your local agendas.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app requests notification permission and schedules local notifications on your device. It does not use remote push notifications or send a device push token to a server. You can change this permission in the system Settings app. Notifications may appear on your lock screen; control previews in your device settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved agendas and session history in the app. Deleting CueTether from your device removes its local app data; offloading an app is different and may keep its data. Manage any device backups and exported copies separately in the services where you saved them. We cannot retrieve or delete local content on your behalf because we do not receive it.

App Store purchase

CueTether is planned as a paid download with no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Apple handles App Store payments, purchase history, and related account information under Apple’s privacy policy. The app does not request your payment details.

This website and support

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or third-party fonts. GitHub may process request information such as IP addresses for hosting and security under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

External websites open only when you follow a link. A support issue you choose to post on GitHub is public and is handled by GitHub. Do not include personal information, meeting content, exported records, passwords, or payment details. We use information you voluntarily post to understand and respond to the request. Your GitHub account name and post are visible to others; GitHub’s controls apply to managing or deleting that content.

Email support

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and message you provide and use them to respond to your request. Emailing support is optional; the app does not send email automatically. Please do not include participant details, meeting content, exported records, passwords, or payment information. For a request concerning your support correspondence, including deletion, contact the same support address.

Questions and changes

CueTether is operated by Ryo Nagaoka. Contact info@lixia.jp for app support or privacy questions. You can also read the support guide. Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
